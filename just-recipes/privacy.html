Privacy policy

Just Recipes for Android · Effective November 5, 2026

The short version: your recipes stay on your device and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The developer has no servers, collects nothing about you, and can't see your recipes.

What the app stores, and where

Recipes, photos, notes, favorites, tags and your shopping list are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Google Drive sync (optional, off unless you turn it on)

  • If you connect a Google account, the app copies your recipes, their photos, notes and favorites to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive (the app data folder), so your other devices can sync with it.
  • The app asks Google for one narrow permission, drive.appdata. It lets the app see only the files it created itself, not anything else in your Drive.
  • Signing in is handled by Google on your device. The app never sees your password.
  • Your shopping list and settings are not synced.
  • To remove the synced copy: in Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps → Just Recipes → Options → Delete hidden app data. To stop the app's access, remove Just Recipes at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Saving recipes from websites

When you save a recipe from a link, the app downloads that page and its photo directly from the website, just as your browser would. That website sees an ordinary visit from your device. Nothing passes through the developer.

Scanning recipe cards

Text in photos is read on your device using Google's ML Kit. Your photos are not uploaded. ML Kit may send Google anonymous statistics about how well it is working, as described in Google's ML Kit documentation.

Sharing and backups

A recipe leaves your device only when you share it, print it or save a backup, and only to the app, printer or place you choose.

If Android's own backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may back up the app's recipe list (not its photos) to your Google account, as it does for other apps.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No accounts with the developer, and no developer servers.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
