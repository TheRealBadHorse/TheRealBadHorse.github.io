Just Recipes

Recipes without the life story. A simple Android app with no ads, no fees and no account.

What it does

  • Save a recipe from any recipe website: share the page to Just Recipes from your browser, and you get just the ingredients, the steps and a photo.
  • Type recipes in, or photograph a recipe card or cookbook page.
  • Find recipes by name, ingredient or tag, and mark your favorites.
  • Cook step by step, with optional read-aloud and tap-to-start timers.
  • Build a shopping list from one or more recipes.
  • Share a recipe as a PDF, as text, or as a Just Recipes file; back up your whole collection.
  • Optionally keep your recipes the same on your phone and tablet through your own Google Drive.

Your recipes stay yours

Your recipes are kept on your device. If you turn on syncing, they are also kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive. There are no servers of ours, nothing is collected about you, and there are no ads. The details are in the privacy policy.
